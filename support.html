Chrono Lane — Support

Chrono Lane is a board game for iPhone and iPad where the whole board ages from the Stone Age to the Future.

Help

  • How to play: the game coaches your first game; How to Play in the title menu explains every rule, and you can long-press any glyph for its name.
  • The Time Traveler Pass: one new game every day is free and the daily challenge is always free. The Pass makes new games unlimited. Bought it on another device or through Family Sharing? Open Settings → Time Traveler Pass → Restore Purchases.
  • Saves: games are saved on your device and resume from Continue. Deleting the app deletes them.
