Chrono Lane — Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

Chrono Lane is a board game for iPhone and iPad made by Archon ("I"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: Chrono Lane does not collect any data about you.

What the game stores

Your games, settings, statistics, achievements progress, profile and Hall of Ages are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are not sent to me or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the game does not do

  • No account or sign-in of my own.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools from any third party.
  • No servers of my own: the game plays entirely offline.

Apple services

  • Game Center (optional): if you are signed in to Game Center, your achievements and leaderboard scores are sent to Apple's Game Center so they can be shown to you and to other players. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; I receive no personal information from it.
  • In-App Purchase: buying the Time Traveler Pass goes through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; I receive no payment or personal details, only the confirmation that the Pass was bought.

Children

Chrono Lane is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
